Choose Week, Month, Year or Schedule from the new grayscale calendar dropdown. Long-press cells for Cut, Copy, Paste, text-only Clear, AutoFill, Group, Ungroup and formatting/color tools. Undo and Redo are at the top left; the bottom toolbar is removed. Hold a group longer to move it between minutes or days. Ungroup selected minutes while retaining notes and colors.
